THE 20-SECOND GUIDE

Two ways to slice a DataFrame.

Both select data, but the thing inside the brackets means something different.

L

.loc[ ] · Label-based

Find rows by their index labels. The ending label is included in a slice.

df.loc[0:4, ['species']]
I

.iloc[ ] · Position-based

Find rows by integer position. Pandas excludes the stop position in slices.

df.iloc[0:5, [0, 4]]
